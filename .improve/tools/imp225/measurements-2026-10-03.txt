REGENERATION COST, and a defect worth knowing: pngdiff.compare() re-decodes both
PNGs on every call, so matrix.py decodes each file N-1 times -- O(N^2) pure-Python
inflate+unfilter passes over ~1.15M pixels. The canonical matrix.py command is
correct and is what produced every number below; it is simply slow (minutes per
group). The matrices in section 2 were printed with a decode-caching wrapper,
/tmp/imp225/nxn.py, which applies the identical predicate. It was validated against
matrix.py on the W2 group and reproduced its numbers exactly (15 pairs, 10 at 0 px,
5 at 329 px). /tmp is not durable; re-create it from the predicate in this header.

matrix.py's --group is a literal filename PREFIX, not a shell glob: it builds
'<dir>/<group>.png'. '--group M1-mobile-headed' therefore matches nothing and
silently prints an empty matrix; you must write 'M1-mobile-headed*'. aggregate.py
absorbs this with an exact-then-wildcard fallback, matrix.py does not.
